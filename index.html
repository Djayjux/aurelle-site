Questions families ask

Are your caregivers vetted?

Yes. Every caregiver completes an identity check, background check, reference checks, and an in-person interview before their first booking.

Do you provide medical or nursing care?

No. Aurelle provides non-medical home care: companionship, supervision, routines and everyday help. We do not give medication, clinical treatment or emergency services.

Can I book just one day?

Yes. One day is the most common way families start. You can request regular days later with the same caregiver when available.

Can I meet the caregiver first?

Yes. For regular plans we arrange a short introduction before the first booking. For same-day requests we share the caregiver’s profile and experience by phone or email.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the visit are free. Later cancellations may incur a fee — see our terms of service.

How do I pay?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and Bank Transfers. We confirm the rate in writing before care begins and send an invoice after the visit.

How do I book?

Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email. We’ll reply to confirm availability, the caregiver and the rate before the visit.
